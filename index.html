Our Resident Welfare Association (RWA) works diligently to maintain high civic standards, clean water supply, efficient underground drainage, and a warm neighborly bond among all resident families.

Community Living

Colony Guidelines & Etiquette

To ensure harmony and peaceful coexistence, all residents and visitors are requested to follow these guidelines.

01

Speed Limit (20 km/h)

Drive cautiously inside colony roads to ensure safety of children and elderly residents. Avoid honking.

02

Waste Segregation

Segregate wet and dry garbage. Door-to-door sanitation vehicles collect waste daily between 7 AM and 9 AM.

03

Quiet Hours (10 PM - 6 AM)

Maintain low sound levels during night hours. Avoid high-volume music and late-night loud machinery.

04

Designated Parking

Park vehicles inside plot premises or along specified curbs without blocking road turnings or neighbor gates.

05

Construction Etiquette

Plot construction materials must not encroach main roads. Construction work is permitted from 8 AM to 6 PM only.

06

Pet Responsibility

Always walk pets on a leash and ensure common walking areas and neighbor compound fronts are kept clean.
